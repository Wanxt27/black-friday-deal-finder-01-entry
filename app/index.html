How the verdict works

Every deal carries its sale price, its usual price, the store, and the day it was checked. The verdict reads the discount only:

  • True deal 50% off or more — a genuine Black Friday cut.
  • Strong deal 30–49% off a real usual price.
  • Solid deal 20–29% off. Worth it if you already wanted it.
  • Mild deal 10–19% off. Fine, not a doorbuster.
  • Weak deal Under 10% off. Barely a sale — wait or compare.
  • Check the fine print The “usual” price is a comparable value, not a past price, so the headline cut may overstate the saving.

An All-time low tag means the source that showed the price called it the lowest it had ever seen for that product.

How these deals were checked

On Friday 27 November 2026 the agent behind this finder read the Black Friday roundups that name each store — 9to5Toys’ Amazon Black Friday Week coverage, BeeBulletin’s deals table, Neeshu, TechRadar’s live blog, The Tech Hacker, PhillyVoice and NewsUnc — and took the sale price and the usual price each one showed. Nothing here is invented: a product, store, price or link appears only if a store or a deals source showed it when it was checked. Each card links to the source that showed its price.

Two honest limits: prices move during Black Friday week, so check the listing before you pay; and Best Buy shows a “comparable value” instead of a past price on some items, which this finder flags rather than hiding.
